01 · Background

Born where disciplines collide.

I²M began as a small group of researchers and practitioners asking a large question: what happens when machine intelligence becomes part of the immersive medium itself?

Today, Intelligence, Immersion & Media welcomes people working across machine learning, human–computer interaction, computer graphics, spatial computing, interactive experiences, and emerging media. We create a shared space for knowledge, experimentation, and collaboration.

Our name evolved

From a digital realm
to an open field.

I²M was founded as Intelligent Immersification in the Metaverse, reflecting the questions that brought our community together. As the field expanded beyond any one platform or technology cycle, we became Intelligence, Immersion & Media—a broader home for AI, HCI, graphics, XR, multimodal systems, creative practice, and whatever comes next.

2024 Official ACM Virtual Chapter

The defining milestone

I²M was started in 2024

By SIGGRAPH Asia 2024, I²M became an official ACM Virtual Chapter: a global community organised around a shared field rather than a geography.

That foundation now continues as Intelligence, Immersion & Media—broad enough to grow with AI, HCI, graphics, spatial computing, and future forms of human–media interaction.

06 Chapter background & governance

Membership

Membership is open to people interested in intelligence, immersion, interactive experiences, and emerging media. Members are encouraged to share knowledge, attend events, and collaborate on exploratory projects. Official membership is managed through ACM and follows the ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.

Chapter structure

I²M operates as an ACM Virtual Chapter with a Chair, Vice-Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Membership Chair, and Web Chair overseeing its activities, communications, membership, and administration.

Meetings & activities

Activities are organised online and alongside conferences and symposiums such as ACM CHI, SIGGRAPH, and SIGGRAPH Asia. Formats include technical sessions, courses, workshops, panels, and collaborative research initiatives.

Finances

The chapter operates on a non-profit basis and may draw on community and sponsor support. Financial activity is reported annually to ACM in accordance with chapter requirements.
