01 · Background

Born where disciplines collide.

I²M began as a small group of researchers and practitioners asking a large question: what happens when machine intelligence becomes part of the immersive medium itself?

Today, the Intelligent Immersion in the Metaverse ACM Chapter welcomes people working across machine learning, human–computer interaction, computer graphics, and related fields. We create a shared space for knowledge, experimentation, and collaboration.

  1. 2023

    AAAI Summer Symposium

    The “AI × Metaverse” track receives the symposium’s highest submission volume and becomes its most-attended track.

  2. 2023

    SIGGRAPH Asia

    A standing-room-only Birds of a Feather session reveals a wider community ready to take the conversation further.

  3. 2024

    I²M is inaugurated

    The initiative becomes an official ACM Virtual Chapter at SIGGRAPH Asia—organised around a shared idea rather than geography.

  4. NOW

    The next dimension

    I²M returns as an open platform for new seminars, workshops, research collaborations, and community-led experiments.

05 Chapter background & governance

Membership

Membership is open to people interested in the intersection of AI and immersive technologies. Members are encouraged to share knowledge, attend events, and collaborate on exploratory projects. Official membership is managed through ACM and follows the ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.

Chapter structure

I²M operates as an ACM Virtual Chapter with a Chair, Vice-Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Membership Chair, and Web Chair overseeing its activities, communications, membership, and administration.

Meetings & activities

Activities are organised online and alongside conferences and symposiums such as ACM CHI, SIGGRAPH, and SIGGRAPH Asia. Formats include technical sessions, courses, workshops, panels, and collaborative research initiatives.

Finances

The chapter operates on a non-profit basis and may draw on community and sponsor support. Financial activity is reported annually to ACM in accordance with chapter requirements.
