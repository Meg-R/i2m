02 · Focus

Three lenses.
One conversation.

Our name is deliberately broad. It gives the community room to follow the next useful question rather than the next buzzword.

01 / INTELLIGENCE

AI that participates.

Agentic and generative AI, multimodal understanding, adaptive systems, and responsible human–AI collaboration.

02 / INTERACTION

Experiences that respond.

Human–computer interaction, immersive and spatial experiences, embodied interfaces, and meaningful user agency.

03 / MULTIMEDIA

Media connected to the world.

Images, video, audio, 3D worlds, simulations, and digital twins that represent and transform real systems.

06 · Our background

Same I²M.
A wider horizon.

Our initiative began with a question about intelligence and immersive digital worlds. That question led us into a much broader field.

I²M was previously known as Intelligent Immersification in the Metaverse. The name reflected the ideas that brought the community together and shaped our early activities, including discussions at SIGGRAPH Asia.

As our work grew to encompass AI-driven multimedia, interactive experiences, and digital twins, “metaverse” no longer described the full scope. Intelligent Interactive Multimedia retains the I²M identity while welcoming more disciplines, applications, and people into the conversation.
